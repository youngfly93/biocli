Top short-list:

- amivantamab
- afatinib
- dacomitinib

- FDA approvals support EGFR-mutant NSCLC as a targetable disease context.

- Amivantamab is relevant for exon 20 insertion NSCLC.
- Afatinib and dacomitinib further confirm tractability.
