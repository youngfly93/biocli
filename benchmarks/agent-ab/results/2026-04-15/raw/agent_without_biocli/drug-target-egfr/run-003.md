# drug-target-egfr run-003

Resolved EGFR to the human epidermal growth factor receptor gene, NCBI Gene 1956. I used official NCBI and FDA sources only, with no biocli invocation.

Key evidence:
- NCBI Gene 1956 identifies EGFR as the human epidermal growth factor receptor.
- FDA approvals show EGFR-mutant NSCLC is a validated therapeutic space for osimertinib, including adjuvant, locally advanced/metastatic, and post-chemoradiation settings.
- FDA approvals also show amivantamab-vmjw is relevant for EGFR exon 20 insertion-mutated NSCLC.
- FDA labeling for erlotinib restricts use to EGFR-mutant NSCLC, supporting target validity even though it is an older option.

Result: EGFR is a viable lung cancer target, but mainly in biomarker-selected NSCLC rather than unselected lung cancer. The most relevant candidates are osimertinib and amivantamab, with erlotinib as historical supporting evidence.
