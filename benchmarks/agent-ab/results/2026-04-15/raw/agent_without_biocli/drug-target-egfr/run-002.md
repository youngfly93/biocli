# drug-target-egfr

Resolved EGFR to the human EGFR gene/protein target (NCBI Gene 1956, UniProt P00533 / HGNC:3236).

Conclusion: EGFR is a highly viable and clinically validated drug target in lung cancer, but mainly in biomarker-selected NSCLC rather than all lung cancers.

Key evidence:
- NCBI Gene and PubChem identify EGFR as the human epidermal growth factor receptor.
- NCI and FDA sources show multiple approved EGFR-directed therapies in NSCLC.
- The actionable NSCLC evidence is strongest for EGFR exon 19 deletions, exon 21 L858R, and T790M.

Shortlist:
- `osimertinib` - preferred EGFR TKI; supported for adjuvant, first-line, and T790M-positive metastatic NSCLC.
- `amivantamab + lazertinib` - first-line targeted regimen for common EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC.
- `amivantamab + carboplatin/pemetrexed` - post-TKI EGFR-mutant NSCLC regimen after resistance emerges.
- `erlotinib / gefitinib / afatinib` - legacy EGFR TKIs that still support tractability, but are generally less preferred than osimertinib.
