# recovery-invalid-study-cbioportal

The requested study id `bogus_study` was invalid. I verified that through the public cBioPortal API, then recovered by selecting the real lung adenocarcinoma cohort `luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` (`Lung Adenocarcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)`).

Key result: TP53 was mutated in 295 of 566 sequenced samples, for a prevalence of about 52.1%.

Recovery note: I re-ran the analysis on the corrected study and pulled a co-mutation clue from the same cohort. TP53 co-occurred with KRAS in 62 samples. Representative TP53 missense variants in the cohort include `V272G` and `Y205C`.

Evidence came from the public cBioPortal study metadata and mutation API, plus the explicit 404 response for `bogus_study`.
