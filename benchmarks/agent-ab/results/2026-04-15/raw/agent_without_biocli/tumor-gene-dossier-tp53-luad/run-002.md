# TP53 LUAD dossier

Study used: `luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` (Lung Adenocarcinoma, TCGA PanCancer Atlas).

Key evidence:
- cBioPortal study metadata reports `566` sequenced LUAD samples.
- TP53 mutation fetch over `luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_all` returned `295` unique mutated samples out of `566`, i.e. `52.1%`.
- Representative TP53 alterations seen in the cohort included `C176Y`, `V272G`, `Y205C`, `K139Rfs*31`, and `G266*`.
- Co-mutation clue: TP53 and KRAS overlapped in `62` LUAD samples; one exemplar was `TCGA-05-4395-01` with `KRAS G12V` plus `TP53 C176Y`.

Recovery notes:
- A direct `/api/cancer-studies/...` lookup returned `404`, so I switched to the documented `/api/studies/{studyId}` endpoint.
- A simple `variant-counts/fetch` request for TP53 returned empty, so I used mutation fetch plus unique sample counting instead.
- One shell request initially hit zsh globbing because the URL was unquoted; it was retried successfully with a quoted POST request.
