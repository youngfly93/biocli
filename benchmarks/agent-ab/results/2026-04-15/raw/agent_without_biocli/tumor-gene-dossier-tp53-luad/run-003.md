# TP53 LUAD dossier

Used the cBioPortal LUAD PanCancer Atlas study `luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018`.

Key evidence:
- TP53 is altered in 295 of 566 sequenced samples, or 52.1%.
- The TP53 mutation spectrum is mostly missense, with recurrent truncating/splice events.
- Representative TP53 alterations include `X125_splice`, `R273L`, `R158L`, `G245V`, `R175H`, `R213*`, and `A159P`.
- Co-mutation example: `TCGA-69-7980-01` carries `KRAS G12A` plus `TP53 A161V` and `TP53 X125_splice`.

No recovery was needed. No failed path was used.
