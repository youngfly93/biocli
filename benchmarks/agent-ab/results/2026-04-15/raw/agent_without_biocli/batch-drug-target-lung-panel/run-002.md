# batch-drug-target-lung-panel run-002

I performed a public-source triage for the five-gene lung panel without using biocli artifacts. The result is a ranked, mutation-specific shortlist suitable for downstream filtering:

- EGFR: top candidate `osimertinib` for exon 19 deletion or exon 21 L858R tumors.
- ALK: top candidate `alectinib` for ALK-positive/rearranged NSCLC.
- MET: top candidate `capmatinib` for MET exon 14 skipping.
- ERBB2: top candidate `sevabertinib` for HER2 (ERBB2) TKD activating mutations; `fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki` is included as a prior approved fallback.
- PIK3CA: deprioritized as exploratory because I found no lung-specific FDA-approved targeted therapy.

Key evidence came from FDA approval/label pages for EGFR, ALK, MET, and ERBB2, plus a public NSCLC PI3K-pathway review for the PIK3CA negative finding. Artifacts were written to:

- `run-002.json`
- `run-002.md`

Recovery note: when a lung-specific PIK3CA drug approval was not found, I recorded the negative result explicitly instead of fabricating a target-drug match.
