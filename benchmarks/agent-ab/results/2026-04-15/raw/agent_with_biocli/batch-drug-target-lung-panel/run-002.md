# batch-drug-target-lung-panel

Ran one multi-gene `compare-genes` pass for `EGFR,ERBB2,PIK3CA,MET,ALK`, then a batched `drug-target` triage with lung disease filtering for the five genes.

Key evidence:
- The comparison run reports 5 genes, 10 shared pathways, 10 shared GO terms, and 9 interaction edges.
- The top GO enrichment term is `Transmembrane Receptor Protein Tyrosine Kinase Signaling Pathway (GO:0007169)` and it includes all five genes.
- The strongest network edges are `EGFR-ERBB2` and `PIK3CA-EGFR` at 0.999, followed by `EGFR-MET` at 0.996.
- The lung-aware top drug candidates are `CAPMATINIB HYDROCHLORIDE` for `MET`, `LAZERTINIB MESYLATE` for `EGFR`, `AFATINIB DIMALEATE` for `ERBB2`, `BRIGATINIB` for `ALK`, and `ALPELISIB` for `PIK3CA`.

Recovery note:
- The first `drug-target` batch attempt through stdin failed as a single batch item; I retried with an explicit temp input file and used the successful JSONL output.

Artifacts:
- `run-002.json`: machine-readable benchmark result for downstream ranking/filtering.
- `run-002.md`: short evidence summary and recovery note.
