# TP53 LUAD benchmark run

Used the hinted cBioPortal study `luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` directly.

Key evidence:
- TP53 altered in 295 of 566 sequenced LUAD samples, `52.12%` mutation prevalence.
- Mutation spectrum was dominated by missense, with additional nonsense, frameshift-deletion, and splice-site/region events.
- Representative TP53 alterations included `X125_splice`, `R158L`, `R273L`, `G245V`, plus exemplar calls such as `V272G`, `K139Rfs*31`, and `R175H`.
- Co-mutation clue: `KRAS` co-mutated with TP53 in 62 samples (`21.02%` of TP53-mutated cases; `10.95%` of the cohort), and was labeled a known driver. `TTN` and `MUC16` were frequent but flagged as likely TMB-associated partners.

No recovery was needed; the hinted study was available and the cohort queries returned structured output from `biocli`.
